Features

Everything you need to focus

Purpose-built tools for students who want more than just a timer.

🏠
Live Study Rooms
Create or join rooms by subject. Real-time chat powered by Socket.io with typing indicators, presence detection, and system events.
⏱
Pomodoro Timer
Configurable focus and break intervals with an audio bell. Sync your timer with the entire room for group focus sessions in real time.
📊
Analytics Dashboard
Visual bar and doughnut charts via Chart.js for daily hours, subject distribution, and weekly goal progress with a ring indicator.
📝
Notes & Resources
Upload files with Multer, share links, write notes. Public or private. Like and bookmark resources from other students in your rooms.
🏆
Leaderboards
Weekly and all-time rankings by study time, session count, and streak length. Gold/silver/bronze podium with a full scrollable table.
🔐
Secure Auth
JWT tokens stored in localStorage, bcrypt password hashing at salt 12, protected routes, and auto-logout on token expiry.

Focus Timer

Stay in flow, together

The Pomodoro technique meets real-time sync. Start a group timer and your whole room focuses at once.

🔔
Audio bell on completion
A crisp Web Audio API tone signals when to work and when to rest — no need to watch the screen.
👥
Group timer sync via Socket.io
Broadcast your timer state to every member of your study room — focus and break together in real time.
📈
Sessions saved to MongoDB
Every completed session is logged with duration, subject, Pomodoro count, and self-rated productivity score.
⚙️
Fully configurable intervals
Set your own work, short break, and long break durations. Preferences are stored in your user profile.
Study Rooms

Never study alone again

Join a live room, chat in real time, and stay accountable with people who share your goals.

🌍
Browse by subject
Filter rooms by Mathematics, Programming, Science, Languages, Business, and more. Jump into a live session instantly.
💬
Real-time chat with Socket.io
Live messaging with typing indicators, system join/leave events, and scrollable chat history stored in MongoDB.
👁
Live presence detection
Active user counts update in real time as students enter and leave. Green dot shows who is currently online.
🔒
Public or private rooms
Public rooms appear in the browser with subject filters. Private rooms are invite-only — ideal for study groups.
